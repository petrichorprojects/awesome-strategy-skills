# Editorial Policy

Awesome Strategy Skills is an editorial product, not an exhaustive index. Inclusion means a maintainer has reviewed the source and can explain why the skill improves a consequential decision.

## Scope

In scope:

- Agent skills and reusable agent workflows for market, product, commercial, organizational, and executive strategy.
- Skills that diagnose a situation, structure choices, expose assumptions, model trade-offs, or produce decision-grade artifacts.
- Collections that materially improve discovery of relevant skills.

Out of scope:

- Generic prompt collections with no bounded workflow.
- Purely tactical skills whose output does not inform a strategic choice.
- Repositories built primarily through bulk generation without credible human review.
- Skills that fabricate research, sources, customer evidence, financial data, or certainty.
- Abandoned, unlicensed, plagiarized, malicious, or deceptively marketed projects.

## Admission standard

Every listed skill is reviewed against eight dimensions:

| Dimension | What reviewers look for |
|---|---|
| Consequential decision | A clear choice, diagnosis, commitment, or prioritization the skill supports. |
| Evidence discipline | Explicit inputs, source handling, assumption labels, and refusal to invent missing evidence. |
| Bounded workflow | Clear activation, exclusions, phases, and completion conditions. |
| Decision-grade output | A specific artifact that can be reviewed, challenged, and used. |
| Pressure testing | Counterarguments, trade-offs, scenarios, failure modes, or adversarial checks. |
| Safety and limits | Honest uncertainty, permission boundaries, and appropriate human ownership. |
| Reproducibility | Enough structure to understand and repeat how an output was produced. |
| Maintenance and licensing | Visible ownership, usable license, current source, and maintenance signal. |

The [Petrichor Strategy Skill Index](evaluation/RUBRIC.md) provides the detailed scoring model. A score is only published after an evaluation record and evidence links are committed. We do not backfill scores from reputation, stars, or README claims.

## Status labels

- **Petrichor Original** — Created by Petrichor Projects. Ownership must be disclosed wherever featured.
- **Reviewed** — A maintainer manually inspected the skill source, scope, outputs, maintenance, and license.
- **Collection** — Included for discovery value. Individual items inside a collection are not automatically endorsed.

Future evaluation statuses may be added after the benchmark harness is public. “Reviewed” does not mean security audited, empirically validated, or endorsed for every use case.

## Conflicts of interest

Petrichor Projects maintains this list and created Resilience Stack. That relationship is disclosed in the README and catalog.

- Self-nominations are allowed but must be disclosed.
- Sponsorship cannot buy inclusion, placement, status, or a favorable evaluation.
- Commercial relationships relevant to an editorial decision must be recorded in the pull request.
- Maintainer-owned work is evaluated using the same published criteria and can be removed.

## Review cadence

- Automated link and catalog checks run on every change and weekly.
- Entries should receive a human review at least every 180 days.
- Material changes to a listed skill may trigger an earlier review.
- Stale, redirected, archived, relicensed, or materially degraded entries can be removed without waiting for the cadence.

## Corrections and appeals

Open an issue with evidence if an entry is mischaracterized, unsafe, stale, improperly licensed, or missing a material disclosure. Authors may appeal a removal or description, but maintainers retain final editorial judgment and will record the reasoning publicly.

## Description standard

Descriptions state what decision or workflow the skill supports. They do not repeat vendor slogans, unverified superlatives, download counts, or claims that cannot be traced to the source.
