# Positioning Strategy Pilot — Evidence and Scores

Scores use the eight-dimension [Petrichor Strategy Skill Index](../../RUBRIC.md). They are provisional pending independent review.

## A. Relevancy Audit — 23 / 24

| Dimension | Score | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Decision specificity | 3 | The output decides to replace the external frame, preserve deployment speed as subordinate proof, names executive owners, and sets rollout boundaries. |
| Evidence discipline | 3 | Every diagnostic score cites fixture evidence; internal and beta proof is qualified; unsupported compliance and accuracy claims are explicitly excluded. |
| Workflow integrity | 3 | Scoring, archaeology, signal inventory, decision, roadmap, indicators, and human boundary form a gated and auditable sequence. |
| Trade-off quality | 3 | Current and candidate frames are compared; the installed-base bridge, competitor-owned language, opportunity costs, and kill criteria are explicit. |
| Adversarial resilience | 3 | The audit looks for unknown competitors, internal narrative conflict, method weakness, stakeholder rejection, and evidence that should disconfirm the proposed frame. |
| Output utility | 3 | The result includes a scorecard, decision, owner-based 90-day roadmap, success measures, stop conditions, and review triggers. |
| Safety and human ownership | 2 | Legal, Security, leadership approvals, claim limits, and human ownership are operational; least-privilege and untrusted-input controls are not part of the workflow. |
| Reproducibility and maintenance | 3 | The pinned repository supplies a rubric, templates, worked examples, evaluations, version history, ownership, and a CC BY 4.0 license. |
