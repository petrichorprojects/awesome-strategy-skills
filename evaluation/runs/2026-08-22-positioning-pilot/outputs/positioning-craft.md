# Positioning Craft Output — HelioDesk

**Pinned skill:** Positioning Craft at `53530efba26431c05ac3fd1dcc5452bdb2fc120e`  
**Decision:** Replace the “AI copilot” frame and validate a governed-resolution position.

## 1. Best for

**Ideal customer**

- Mid-market SaaS companies with 100–1,000 employees.
- Policy-sensitive, multilingual, or high-escalation support operations.
- VP or Head of Support as champion, with Risk, Security, Operations, or COO influence.
- Teams seeking more automation without surrendering proof, approval, or human override.

**Not for**

- Small teams buying the cheapest ticket-deflection bot.
- Enterprises that require certifications HelioDesk's newest governance features do not yet cover.
- Buyers seeking a blanket “hallucination-free” or compliance guarantee.

## 2. Category

**Recommended category hypothesis:** Governed AI support platform.

**Alternative to test:** Defensible AI resolution platform.

**Category context:** Software that automates customer-support resolution while grounding answers in approved policy, routing exceptions, preserving human control, and recording evidence.

The existing “AI support copilot” category should not remain primary: only one of ten prospects uses it, and the analyst now treats “copilot vendor” as a limiting legacy label. ResolveGrid's “resolution governance control plane” should not be copied.

## 3. Alternatives

| Alternative | Why buyers choose it | Where HelioDesk can be different |
|---|---|---|
| Intercom Fin | Integrated customer-service stack and resolution-rate narrative. | Policy-grounded control without requiring the full Intercom environment. |
| Zendesk AI | Installed-suite breadth and familiar workflows. | Faster deployment with more explicit answer provenance and escalation control. |
| ResolveGrid | Governance, approval, and auditability. | Join defensibility with lower implementation risk and established support automation. |
| Current approach | Keep HelioDesk as a fast ticket-deflection copilot. | Low switching cost, but increasingly commoditized and misaligned with buyer language. |

## 4. Unique value

HelioDesk's plausible differentiation is not “more AI.” It is the combination of:

1. Fast deployment.
2. Policy-grounded answers.
3. Human approval and escalation controls.
4. Emerging auditability and cost-per-resolution evidence.

**Why that matters:** Support leaders can automate more work while giving risk stakeholders a traceable reason to approve the system.

## One-sentence positioning hypothesis

> For policy-sensitive mid-market support teams that need to automate without losing control, HelioDesk is a governed AI support platform that grounds answers, routes exceptions, and preserves an audit trail. Unlike generic copilots, it combines rapid deployment with evidence a cross-functional buying group can inspect.

## What must be tested before adoption

- Interview twelve recent buyers across wins, losses, and active evaluations.
- Test the current frame against both candidate category hypotheses without identifying the author.
- Require a candidate to win at least seven of twelve first-choice votes and remain credible across Support and risk stakeholders.
- Reject or revise any frame that implies certified compliance, universal safety, or independently proven accuracy.
- Pilot the chosen position in one policy-sensitive segment for 30 days before a complete market rollout.
