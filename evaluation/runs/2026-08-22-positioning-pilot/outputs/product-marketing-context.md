## Product overview

**One-liner:** HelioDesk helps policy-sensitive support teams automate customer resolutions while retaining grounding, escalation control, and evidence.

**What it does:** HelioDesk is B2B customer-support automation software. It combines policy-grounded answers, multi-model routing, human approval and escalation controls, emerging audit logs, and cost-per-resolution reporting.

**Current product category:** AI support copilot.  
**Provisional category direction:** Governed AI support platform or defensible AI resolution platform.  
**Product type:** B2B SaaS.  
**Business model:** Subscription software; price and packaging were not supplied.

## Target audience

**Target companies:** Mid-market SaaS companies with 100–1,000 employees, especially policy-sensitive, multilingual, regulated, or high-escalation support environments.

**Decision-makers and influencers:** VP or Head of Support, COO, Security, Risk, Support Operations, and potentially Finance for cost-per-resolution evaluation.

**Primary use case:** Automate support resolution without losing policy control, human override, or auditability.

**Jobs to be done:**

- Resolve more customer requests at a defensible quality level.
- Prove which policy grounded an answer and how exceptions were handled.
- Give Support speed while satisfying cross-functional risk review.
- Measure the cost and quality of each resolved case.

## Problems and pain points

**Core problem:** The buying problem has shifted from generating answers to controlling and defending automated resolutions.

**Why alternatives fall short:**

- Generic copilots make deployment easy but can leave proof and governance unclear.
- Installed suites offer breadth but may not expose the control or provenance a cross-functional group demands.
- Governance-first competitors can add control but may impose a heavier implementation story.

**What it costs customers:** Longer approvals, custom compliance work, escalation risk, uncertain unit economics, and loss of confidence in automation.

**Emotional tension:** Support leaders want the leverage of autonomous resolution but do not want to be responsible for an answer they cannot defend.

## Competitive landscape

**Direct:**

- Intercom Fin — wins with integration and resolution-rate language.
- Zendesk AI — wins with suite breadth and installed familiarity.
- ResolveGrid — wins with governance, policy graphs, and auditability.

**Secondary:** Internal automation and custom model-routing systems.

**Indirect:** Human-only support operations or delaying AI adoption.

## Differentiation

**Provisional differentiators:**

- Generally available policy grounding.
- Generally available human approval and escalation controls.
- Deployment speed and lower implementation risk.
- Audit logs and cost reporting moving through beta.

**How HelioDesk may be better:** It can join control and defensibility to a faster support-automation deployment path.

**Why customers currently choose it:** Evidence is fragmented across deployment speed, cost reduction, routing controls, and implementation risk. Leadership must validate one shared core rather than present all four as equal.
