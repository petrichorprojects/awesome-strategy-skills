## Positioning decision

**Replace the external “AI support copilot” frame. Preserve the installed-base promise as a subordinate benefit.**

Provisional strategic hypothesis:

> HelioDesk is a governed AI support platform for policy-sensitive mid-market SaaS teams that need fast automation and resolutions they can defend.

This is a hypothesis, not final copy. “Governed AI support platform” should be tested against “defensible AI resolution” and the current frame. “Resolution governance control plane” is excluded because ResolveGrid already owns it in active deals.

### Best fit

- Mid-market SaaS with policy-sensitive, multilingual, regulated, or high-escalation support operations.
- A VP Support champion facing approval from Risk, Security, Operations, or the COO.
- Teams that want automation speed but cannot accept untraceable answers.

### Not best fit

- Small teams buying only low-cost ticket deflection.
- Buyers demanding blanket compliance or accuracy guarantees HelioDesk cannot evidence.
- Enterprises requiring certifications the current governance features do not yet cover.
