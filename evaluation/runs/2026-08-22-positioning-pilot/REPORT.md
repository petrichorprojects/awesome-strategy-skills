# Positioning Strategy Pilot — Report

## Finding

For the HelioDesk positioning-decay decision, **Relevancy Audit produced the most complete decision system**: it diagnosed the severity of decay, preserved an evidence trail, compared strategic alternatives, assigned owners, and defined 90-day tests and stop conditions.

The other skills remain useful for different jobs:

- **Positioning Craft** is the fastest route to a compact positioning hypothesis and validation plan.
- **Product Marketing Context** is the strongest shared context layer for downstream marketing work, but it does not natively decide whether a position should be preserved, refreshed, or replaced.

This is a task-fit result, not a universal ranking.

## What drove the result

### Relevancy Audit

The output tied a 26/30 decay diagnosis to market evidence, made the replace-versus-refresh decision explicit, preserved the installed-base benefit, excluded a competitor-owned category, and converted uncertainty into research gates. Its main limitation is operational safety: human approval and claim boundaries are present, but least-privilege and untrusted-input controls are outside the skill's primary design.

### Positioning Craft

The output reached a credible category hypothesis with a useful best-fit/not-for boundary and a compact proof table. The native workflow is easy to execute, but it relies on the operator to diagnose the severity of change, structure disconfirming evidence, assign owners, and govern the rollout.

### Product Marketing Context

The output created the broadest reusable artifact: product, personas, switching forces, competition, language, proof, and open decisions. That breadth is valuable after leadership has made a positioning choice. On its own, however, it documents options more readily than it adjudicates them or exposes their opportunity costs.

## Recommended operating sequence

1. Run **Relevancy Audit** when the core question is whether the current position still fits market reality.
2. Use **Positioning Craft** to compress the chosen direction into a buyer-testable canvas.
3. Store the validated decision in **Product Marketing Context** so downstream launch, content, sales, and growth work share the same truth.

The skills are complements when used in that order.

## Critical-failure check

No output fabricated evidence, made an unqualified compliance or safety claim, hid the decision, or removed human ownership. No critical failure was triggered.
