# HelioDesk Evidence Pack

**As-of date:** 2026-08-15  
**Company:** HelioDesk  
**Business:** B2B SaaS for customer-support automation  
**ARR:** $24 million  
**Primary segment:** Mid-market SaaS companies with 100–1,000 employees  
**Current buyer:** VP or Head of Customer Support  

## Decision to make

Leadership must decide whether to preserve, refresh, or replace the positioning established in January 2023. The company has 90 days to improve market clarity without alienating its installed customer base.

## Current positioning

> “The AI copilot for every support team. Deflect more tickets with instant, accurate answers.”

The position was built when generative-AI access and fast deployment were novel. The homepage, pitch deck, paid campaigns, and sales narrative still lead with ticket deflection, setup speed, and an “AI copilot” category frame.

Leadership discussed the increase in compliance and risk-team questions in January 2026 but did not act. The stated reason was that Support remained the budget owner and “risk slows the deal.” Sales compensation rewards qualified pipeline and close speed; it does not reward identifying new buying-group members or governance requirements.

## Internal conviction check

Five leaders answered, independently, why the best-fit customer chooses HelioDesk:

- CEO: “We deploy useful support AI faster than anyone.”
- CRO: “We reduce cost per ticket without replacing the support stack.”
- CPO: “Our routing and escalation controls make AI safer.”
- VP Marketing: “We are the easiest enterprise support copilot to adopt.”
- Top account executive: “Customers trust us because implementation risk is low.”

The CEO and VP Marketing share a speed-and-adoption core. The other three emphasize different outcomes.

## Product proof available now

- A policy-grounding engine is generally available.
- Human approval and escalation controls are generally available.
- An audit log is in beta with four customers.
- In an internal audited sample of 18,400 answers, 99.3% cited an approved policy source. Methodology has not been independently validated.
- Four beta customers reduced escalations by a median 41%; sample selection was not randomized.
- Cost-per-resolution reporting is in beta.
- HelioDesk does not yet have SOC 2 evidence covering every new governance feature.

## Constraints

- The installed base bought ticket deflection and cannot be told that the old value is obsolete.
- ResolveGrid already owns “resolution governance control plane” language in active deals.
- The company cannot make unqualified compliance, safety, or accuracy guarantees.
- Leadership can change messaging, sales qualification, proof packaging, and beta priorities within 90 days.
- A full product rebuild or new enterprise certification cannot be completed within 90 days.
