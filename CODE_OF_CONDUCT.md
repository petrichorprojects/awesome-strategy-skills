# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, evidence-oriented community. Contributors should:

- Critique ideas, workflows, evidence, and claims rather than people.
- Disclose relationships that could affect editorial judgment.
- Welcome correction and update conclusions when evidence changes.
- Avoid harassment, intimidation, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and unwanted attention.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, licenses, and security-reporting norms.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or participants whose behavior undermines a safe and constructive project. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact information at [petrichorgrowth.com](https://petrichorgrowth.com). Reports will be handled with discretion and reviewed for conflicts of interest.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and in public spaces when someone is representing this project.
